## Product evidence and boundaries

- **Onlook**: [official site](https://www.onlook.com/), [official docs](https://docs.onlook.com/), [official repository](https://github.com/onlook-dev/onlook). Visual React editing and AI code assistance are documented. The current company site offers early access, while the open-source versions have independent setup instructions. No public launch date is asserted. The Product Hunt `/products/onlook` result was rejected because it describes a different news-reader product. Kilo was researched but excluded because its current scale and Anaconda positioning were a weaker match for this selection.
- **Sim**: [workflow overview](https://www.sim.ai/workflows), [official docs](https://docs.sim.ai/introduction), [pricing](https://www.sim.ai/pricing), [Product Hunt product](https://www.producthunt.com/products/sim-studio), [2026-07-10 daily leaderboard](https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard/daily/2026/7/10). The dated PH launch is recorded as a PH event, not the date the product first existed. Open source does not imply free hosting, model calls, or external integrations.
- **Omi**: [official store](https://www.omi.me/), [help-center overview](https://help.omi.me/en/articles/13135007-overview), [pricing](https://www.omi.me/pages/pricing). The official page currently accepts orders for the pendant. Availability and shipping have not been tested by placing an order. Companion-app requirements and recording consent are stated. No medical, privacy-certification, battery-life, or performance claims are repeated as independent findings.
- **Looki L1**: [official product page](https://www.looki.ai/products/looki-l1), [product overview](https://www.looki.ai/pages/looki-l1), [app downloads](https://www.looki.ai/pages/download). Current product page has Add to cart and supports iOS/Android companion apps. Interval lifelogging is distinguished from continuous all-day video. We did not place an order or test recording quality.
- **Rosebud AI**: [official game creator](https://rosebud.ai/ai-game-creator), [official tutorial](https://www.play.rosebud.ai/tutorial), [pricing](https://www.play.rosebud.ai/pricing). Descriptions, browser previews, chat edits, credits, and plan-dependent commercial rights are documented. A PH result for the similarly named Rosebud journaling app was rejected.
- **Odyssey**: [current official site](https://odyssey.systems/), [Odyssey-3 announcement dated 2026-09-15](https://odyssey.systems/introducing-odyssey-3), [experience portal linked by the official site](https://experience.odyssey.systems/). The former odyssey.world domain redirects to odyssey.systems. The experience portal returned 403 to the read-only retrieval tool, so we did not claim to have tried a session or confirm public capacity. The catalog explicitly labels it experimental and notes access limits. The cover is official brand artwork, not a fabricated screenshot. No PH launch date is asserted.
- **Granola**: [official site](https://www.granola.ai/), [current pricing and platform comparison](https://www.granola.ai/pricing), [Product Hunt profile](https://www.producthunt.com/products/granola). Current official pricing lists desktop, iOS, and Android apps and limited-history free use. We did not run real meeting transcription. No launch date is added because this entry is not framed as a current release event.
